🎯 Sentiment Scoring & Rating Calibration

Audience reactions are evaluated via natural language polarity scoring, mapping contextual sentiment directly to numerical reception:

Positive Classification (≥ +0.05)

High praise, enthusiasm, exceptional craftsmanship (e.g. "Masterpiece!" → +0.66). Corresponds to 8-10 ratings.

Neutral Classification (-0.05 to +0.05)

Balanced, mild, or factual impressions (e.g. "Average film." → 0.00). Corresponds to 6-7 ratings.

Negative Classification (≤ -0.05)

Criticism, dissatisfaction, poor pacing (e.g. "Waste of time." → -0.42). Corresponds to 1-5 ratings.
